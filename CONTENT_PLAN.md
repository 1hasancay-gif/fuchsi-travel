# Fuchsi Travel – A1 curriculum design

## Scope
The A1 route is organized around CEFR A1 can-do outcomes: familiar everyday expressions, introducing oneself and others, asking/answering personal questions, and simple interaction with slow, clear support. Goethe-Zertifikat A1 Start Deutsch 1 covers listening, reading, writing and speaking, so the app deliberately rotates all four skills instead of teaching only word/translation pairs.

## 20-unit route
1. Selamlaşma — greetings, formal/informal, pronouns
2. Kendini Tanıtma — name, age, residence, sein, W-questions
3. Ülkeler & Diller — kommen aus, wohnen in, languages
4. Aile — haben, possessives, plurals
5. Sayılar & Telefon — 0–100, phone numbers, prices
6. Saat & Tarih — clock, days, months, appointments
7. Günlük Rutin — regular present-tense verbs, sentence order
8. Ev & Oda — articles, plural, es gibt
9. Şehir & Yön — places, left/right/straight, basic imperatives
10. Ulaşım — bus/train/ticket, mit/zu review
11. İş & Okul — jobs, schedules, können/müssen intro
12. Alışveriş — wants/requests, accusative intro
13. Yeme & İçme — food/drink, gern, kein/keine, möchten
14. Restoran — ordering, menu, bill, polite requests
15. Sağlık — body, symptoms, doctor/pharmacy phrases
16. Hava & Mevsimler — weather, seasons, temperature
17. Hobi & Boş Zaman — likes/dislikes, frequency
18. Seyahat & Otel — arrival/departure, room, simple booking language
19. Mesaj & Randevu — arranging, cancelling, meeting times
20. A1 Final — integrated listening/reading/writing/speaking mock

## Lesson engine
Each micro-lesson is built as: **meaning → model → listening → retrieval → controlled production → feedback → transfer**. A word/collocation is revisited after increasing intervals rather than massed repetition; this follows evidence that spacing has a medium-to-large effect on second-language learning and that retrieval practice strengthens later recall.

## Adaptive memory
Every item gets a simple mastery state. Wrong or hesitant answers re-enter review sooner; stable items are delayed. The goal is not to maximize taps/XP but durable retrieval.
